Official Rules & Transparency

Everything you need to know about our hourly selection mechanism.

🎲 Equal Opportunity Random Draw

Every eligible entry in an active round has an exact, equal mathematical probability of being selected.

We do NOT prioritize or rank profiles based on follower counts, subscriber metrics, social clout, bidding amount, or submission time. All eligible entries in the round pool are treated with identical weight.

⏱️ 60-Minute Hourly Rounds

  • Each round runs for exactly 60 minutes (3,600 seconds).
  • Entries submitted during an active round participate exclusively in that round.
  • When the round countdown reaches zero, the server closes the round and selects up to 3 winners.
  • A new round automatically begins immediately following round conclusion.

💳 Paid Entry via Razorpay

Participation requires an entry fee of ₹{{ entry_fee_inr|default(49)|int }} per entry, processed securely through Razorpay.

  • An entry only becomes active and eligible after cryptographic signature verification succeeds on the server.
  • Unpaid, pending, or cancelled payment attempts are never entered into the draw.
  • Duplicate submissions of the same profile URL in the same round are prevented.

🏆 Top 3 Featured Creators

At the end of each round:

  • 0 entries: Round concludes with no winners.
  • 1 entry: 1 Gold winner selected.
  • 2 entries: 1 Gold and 1 Silver winner selected.
  • 3 or more entries: 3 winners selected without replacement (Gold, Silver, Bronze).

The winners remain prominently featured on the homepage for the entire following 60 minutes, and their records are permanently preserved in the Winners Archive.
