Privacy Policy

Privacy-first design • No user accounts • Minimal data collection

1. No Accounts Required

indobid.lol is built with privacy as a foundational principle. Normal visitors do not create user accounts, do not provide passwords, and do not maintain user profiles on our system.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an entry to participate in an hourly draw, we collect:

  • Display Name: The name you choose to represent your profile.
  • Platform: The social network or website type (e.g., X, GitHub, YouTube, Website).
  • Public Profile URL: The public link you submit for showcase.

3. Privacy-Conscious Visitor Tracking

To accurately report unique daily visitors without compromising user privacy, we generate a one-way cryptographic hash of your temporary IP address combined with the current UTC date and a secret server salt. Your raw, personal IP address is never stored permanently in our database.

4. Payment Processing via Razorpay

Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We receive transaction verification tokens (Order ID and Payment ID) to validate your payment. We never see, collect, or store your credit card numbers, debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.

5. Data Retention & Transparency

Winners' display names, platforms, and public links are permanently preserved on our public Winners Archive for platform transparency and auditability.
