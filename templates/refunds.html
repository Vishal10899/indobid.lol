Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear guidelines regarding entry fees and digital transactions

1. Nature of Real-Time Digital Entries

indobid.lol operates high-cadence, 60-minute profile discovery rounds. Upon successful server-side verification of your Razorpay payment, your entry is immediately added to the active round pool and becomes eligible for random winner selection.

2. Non-Refundable Policy

Because entries are immediately placed into the active pool with equal mathematical probability of winning, entry fees are strictly non-refundable once confirmed.

3. Technical Errors & Double Charges

If your payment was debited by Razorpay but due to a network interruption or server error your entry was not created in the database, please contact support at support@indobid.lol with your Razorpay Payment ID.

Verified duplicate transactions or unfulfilled entry orders will be refunded in full within 5–7 business days to the original payment source.

4. Disqualified Entries

Entries disqualified by administration for violating terms (e.g. promoting malware, phishing, or illegal content) forfeit their entry fee and are not eligible for a refund.
