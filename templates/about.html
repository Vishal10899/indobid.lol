About indobid.lol

An hourly profile-discovery platform built to feature creators, developers, and builders.

01

Zero Friction • No User Accounts

We believe the modern web has too many sign-up forms, confirmation emails, and forgotten passwords. On indobid.lol, visitors never need to register an account. You simply enter your display name, platform, and public profile link, complete the entry fee through Razorpay, and your entry is instantly eligible.

02

The Hourly Discovery Flow

STEP 1

Submit & Pay

Enter your public creator profile and complete the ₹49 entry fee via Razorpay.

STEP 2

Hourly Draw

When the 60-minute countdown concludes, 3 eligible profiles are randomly selected.

STEP 3

Featured Spotlight

Gold, Silver, and Bronze winners are featured on the homepage for the entire following hour.

03

Authoritative Timestamp Engine

indobid.lol runs an on-demand, timestamp-based round engine specifically engineered to run efficiently on Render Free and PostgreSQL without background queues.

  • The backend and PostgreSQL strictly determine round completion and winner selection. The browser is never authoritative.
  • Idempotent concurrency guarantees that multiple simultaneous incoming requests cannot create duplicate winners or desynchronize rounds.
  • Historical winners are permanently archived and accessible on the Winners page.
