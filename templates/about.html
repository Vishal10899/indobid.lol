About indobid.lol

An hourly profile-discovery platform designed to give creators, developers, and builders organic exposure.

01

No Accounts. No Passwords.

We believe the modern web has too many sign-up forms, confirmation emails, and forgotten passwords. On indobid.lol, visitors never need an account. You simply enter your display name, platform, and public profile URL, and you are immediately entered into the draw.

02

The Hourly Flow

STEP 1

Submit Profile

Submit your X, YouTube, GitHub, TikTok, or Website URL during any active round.

STEP 2

Draw Executes

At the top of the hour, the backend randomly selects 3 eligible participants: Gold, Silver, and Bronze.

STEP 3

Featured on Top

The 3 winners take center stage on the homepage for the entire following hour.

03

Deterministic & Sleep-Proof Engine

indobid.lol runs an on-demand, timestamp-based round engine engineered to run seamlessly on Render Free web services and PostgreSQL.

  • The backend and PostgreSQL determine round completion and winner selection. The browser is never authoritative.
  • Idempotent concurrency guarantees that multiple simultaneous incoming requests cannot create duplicate winners or desynchronized rounds.
  • Historical winners are permanently archived and accessible on the Winners page.
