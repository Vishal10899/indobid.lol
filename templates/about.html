How It Works

  1. 1 Submit your link: Enter your display name, platform, and public URL.
  2. 2 Pay {{ settings.currency_symbol|default('$') }}{{ settings.listing_price|int if settings.listing_price.is_integer() else settings.listing_price }}: Complete payment securely via Razorpay.
  3. 3 Join the 60-minute round: Upon payment verification, your listing enters the active round.
  4. 4 Hourly draw: At the end of 60 minutes, 3 listings are randomly selected (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
  5. 5 Homepage Top 3: Selected winners appear prominently on the homepage for the next hour.
  6. 6 Permanent archive: All previous winners remain permanently in the Winners archive.
"Every paid listing has an equal chance of being selected."

There is NO voting, NO ranking based on followers, NO algorithmic scoring, and NO popularity score. Every eligible paid listing gets an equal random probability.
